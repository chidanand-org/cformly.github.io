Compress a photo for the FRRO portal

The portal rejects guest photos over 50 KB. This free tool shrinks any photo below the limit in your browser — the file never leaves your device.

How it works

  1. Choose the guest photo from your phone or computer.
  2. The tool resizes it and re-encodes it below your target size — entirely on this page, offline.
  3. Download the compressed JPEG and attach it on the portal.

No upload, ever: this page has no server and no tracking. The photo is processed by your own browser and stays on your device.
