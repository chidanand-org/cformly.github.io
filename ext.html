The extension: fill inside your own session

The free Cformly browser extension writes the reviewed guest fields into the FRRO portal form — in your own tab, under your own login. It never touches credentials or personal data.

How the fill works

  1. You send the reviewed fields from the Cformly app to yourself — email or WhatsApp. The details travel through the message you send, never through our servers.
  2. On the FRRO portal page, you click the Cformly button and press Fill. The extension reads the payload only on that explicit click.
  3. It writes the fields into the form open in your own browser tab, where you can watch and correct every one.
  4. You type the security code, attach the photo, and press Save. The filing is yours — Cformly never submits anything.

What the extension never does

  • It never stores credentials or guest PII — nothing persists after the fill.
  • It never writes to your clipboard, and it never sends anything to Cformly servers — there is no relay.
  • It operates on one site only: the official FRRO portal page you have open.
  • It never solves the CAPTCHA or attaches the photo — those stay with the host, as the portal requires.

Verified form mappings

The portal's layout changes sometimes. The fill instructions the extension loads (the form mapping) carry an Ed25519 signature checked against a public key built into the extension — if a mapping doesn't verify, the extension refuses to use it.

Fail closed, by design: an unverifiable mapping is never applied.
