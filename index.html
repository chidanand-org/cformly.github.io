How it works

Three moves, one register.

From the guest at the counter to the filed form on the portal — the same three steps, every time.

Capture

Point the phone camera at the passport and visa. On-device OCR reads the machine-readable zone — no typing, works offline at the desk.

2

Review

Every field lands on one screen with confidence flags. Fix what OCR flagged, add arrival and stay details, and snap the guest photo at the portal's 50 KB limit.

3

Send

Open the FRRO portal in a browser and enter your handoff code — the Cformly extension fills the form. No extension handy? Copy the fields; the Tier 1 clipboard path works on any machine.

Privacy

Guest data stays on the property.

Passports and visas are personal data. Cformly is built so that data never needs to leave the front desk — and when it does, it stays in India.

On-device OCR

Passports and visas are read on the phone with open-source software. No third-party AI services, no cloud documents.

Encrypted at rest

SQLCipher on the device, servers in India. Biometric or PIN lock, screen privacy, and clipboard hygiene are built in.

Human in the loop

The CAPTCHA and the photo attachment stay with the host. Cformly fills the form; the host files it.

Your data, your say

Export or delete everything from Settings at any time — your rights under the DPDP Act 2023.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is Cformly a government app?
No. Cformly is an independent filing assistant. The host remains legally responsible for accurate and timely filing, and Cformly is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FRRO or the Government of India.
Who solves the CAPTCHA on the portal?
The host, always. The extension fills the form fields; the human solves the CAPTCHA and attaches the photo before submitting.
Where does guest data live?
Passports and visas are read on the device or on Cformly's own servers in India — no third-party AI services. Guest data is stored encrypted and can be exported or deleted from Settings at any time.
Does it work offline?
Capture and review work fully offline. Filling the portal needs a browser and the extension; the Tier 1 clipboard path covers any other machine.
What is the 24-hour rule?
Foreign guests must be registered with the FRRO within 24 hours of check-in. Cformly shows a countdown from the moment a guest is captured, so the deadline never sneaks up on the night shift.
Which languages does it support?
English and Hindi, switchable from settings.
