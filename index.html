How it works

Four steps from guest arrival to filed Form C.

1

Scan

Point the camera at the guest's passport and visa. Cformly extracts all fields via OCR.

2

Review

Check the extracted data, add any missing fields like arrival details. Confidence flags highlight what to double-check.

3

Fill

Get a handoff code. The browser extension auto-fills the FRRO portal form — no copy-pasting.

4

Submit

Review on the portal, upload the photo, and Save. Cformly captures the Application ID for your records.
